SPATIAL MODULARITY MANDATE
==========================

1. COMPONENT ISOLATION
- Systems must not directly access internal room state.
- Use the CommsBus for inter-system signaling.

2. DATA-DRIVEN TOPOLOGY
- All room connections must be defined in roomDefinitions.json.
- Socket tags determine physical compatibility.

3. VALIDATION FIRST
- No ship can be instantiated without passing the LayoutValidator.
- Valid rules: 100% reachability, no dead corridors, valid socket pairs.
